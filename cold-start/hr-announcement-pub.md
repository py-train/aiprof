# Case title
HR announcement: return-to-office and mandatory AI training completion

## Scenario brief
HR needs to send a company-wide announcement about a new workplace policy. The company is moving away from the full WFH model. Starting next month, employees are expected to work from the office 4 days per week. In addition, all employees must complete the mandatory AI training modules before the end of the quarter.

## Likely improvement path

- Audience: all employees.
- Tone: respectful, clear, firm, non-threatening.
- Objective: announce policy change and drive compliance.
- Constraints: mention office attendance requirement and AI training deadline.
- Output format: email announcement with subject line, short paragraphs, and bullet points for key actions.

## Stronger prompt version
“Draft a company-wide HR announcement for all employees. The company is transitioning away from full WFH, and starting next month everyone is expected to work from the office 4 days per week. Also, all employees must complete the mandatory AI training modules before the end of the quarter. Write in a professional, respectful, and firm tone. Keep it clear, concise, and easy to understand. Include a subject line, short intro, key policy points, and a closing that encourages cooperation.”
.

.

.

.

.

.
## Even better prompt version
“Write a company-wide HR email announcing two policy changes: 1) employees are now required to work from the office 4 days per week starting next month, and 2) all employees must complete the mandatory AI training modules before the end of the quarter. The tone should be professional, empathetic, and firm. Acknowledge that this is a change for many people. Keep the message concise, avoid jargon, and make the key actions easy to scan. Include a subject line, a short explanation of why this is being introduced, bullet points for the actions required, and a closing note that encourages a smooth transition.”
